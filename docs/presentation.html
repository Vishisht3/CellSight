The Problem & The Solution

Two Disconnected Worlds, Both Failing the Industrial EV Transition
11 / 12
⚠ The Problem: Two Disconnected Worlds
🚛 Fleet Operators: No APM Parity

Industrial machinery has decades of APM tooling. EV batteries have none. Fleet managers are flying blind on degradation, thermal risk, and replacement timing — leading to unplanned downtime and accelerated degradation from suboptimal charging.

🏭 EV Manufacturers: Multi-Tier Supply Chain Opacity

Critical battery materials pass through 3+ supplier tiers with no unified risk view. Concentration, geopolitical, quality, and compliance risks are siloed. When field packs degrade faster than expected, there's no mechanism to trace back to the originating batch or supplier.

🔗 The Gap Nobody Fills

Field performance data sits with fleet operators. Supply chain data sits with manufacturers. The correlation that would improve both sides — linking real-world degradation to the batch and supplier that caused it — is never made.

✓ CellSight — One Platform, Two Agents, One Shared Thread
APM Agent → Fleet Operations
  • State-of-Health computation with confidence scoring
  • Remaining Useful Life projection in days and cycles
  • Thermal event detection in under 60 seconds
  • Charge pattern optimisation (20–80% SoC window)
  • Ranked maintenance trigger queue
Supply Chain Agent → Manufacturers
  • 3-tier material traceability (lot → batch → pack → asset)
  • Concentration, geopolitical, quality, compliance risk scoring
  • Composite 0–100 supplier risk score with 4-dimension breakdown
  • Full chain query in under 3 seconds
🔗 Correlation Agent — The Core Differentiator

The only platform that links real-world field degradation rates back to the originating cell batch and raw material supplier — creating a closed feedback loop visible simultaneously to both fleet managers and supply chain managers.
